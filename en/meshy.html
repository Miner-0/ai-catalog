Meshy: Instant 3D Model Creation with AI

Meshy is an advanced neural network designed specifically to automate three-dimensional modeling. It significantly accelerates the workflow for indie game developers, architects, and CG designers.

What can this AI do?

  • Generate fully textured 3D models from text descriptions (Text-to-3D).
  • Turn ordinary flat 2D images and photos into 3D objects (Image-to-3D).
  • Automatically create high-quality texture maps for pre-made 3D models (AI Texturing).
  • Export finished files into popular formats (fbx, obj, glb) compatible with Blender, Unreal Engine, and Unity.

How to use it for free?

After a simple registration, Meshy provides users with daily free creative credits. This amount is completely sufficient to generate dozens of unique game assets, interior items, or characters every month.
