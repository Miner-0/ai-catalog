Perplexity AI: A Smart Alternative to Traditional Search Engines

Perplexity AI is an innovative search engine that completely changes how we find information online. Instead of presenting an endless list of websites and links, the AI analyzes the web itself and provides a single, accurate, and verified answer.

Why is it more convenient than regular search?

  • Citations and Sources: The AI attaches a clickable number-link to the source website for every claim it makes. No fabrications.
  • "Pro" Mode: Allows you to ask follow-up questions to dig deep into the core of any complex topic.
  • File Uploads: You can upload a PDF document or a book directly into the search bar and ask the AI to generate a brief summary.
