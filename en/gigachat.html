GigaChat: The Main Russian-Language AI Assistant from Sber

GigaChat is a flagship neural network developed by Sber. Its main advantage over foreign counterparts is its deep understanding of context, humor, idioms, and cultural nuances of the Russian language.

What Makes GigaChat the Perfect Choice?

  • No Restrictions: The service is fully accessible in Russia and the CIS, does not require a VPN or foreign phone numbers for registration, and works completely free of charge.
  • In-Chat Image Generation: Thanks to its integration with the Kandinsky neural network, you can ask right during the conversation: “Visualize this” or “Draw an illustration for this text.”
  • Assistant for Study and Work: It writes essays, summarizes long texts, generates content plans, and helps school and university students tackle complex problems.
