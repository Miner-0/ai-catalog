Adobe Firefly: Commercial-Grade Artificial Intelligence

Adobe Firefly is a family of generative AI models created by the developers of the legendary image editor, Photoshop. The main difference between Firefly and its competitors is that the AI is trained exclusively on licensed images and public domain stocks, making its graphics completely safe for commercial use.

Key Features of Adobe Firefly:

  • Generative Fill: Allows you to work true magic. You can select an area on an existing photo and ask the AI to: *“Dress the person in a leather jacket”* or *“Add mountains to the background.”* The result is seamlessly integrated, perfectly accounting for shadows and lighting.
  • Text Effects: Transforms ordinary letters into 3D art pieces. You can apply a style to text, such as making letters look like they are made "of fluffy fur" or "of molten metal."
  • Generative Recolor: Indispensable for vector designers. It allows you to instantly change the color schemes of logos and icons to match different brand styles using simple text prompts.
