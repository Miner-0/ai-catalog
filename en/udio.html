Udio AI: Studio-Grade Music Track Generation

Udio AI is an innovative neural network capable of turning a text description into a finished song. The model is famous for its incredibly clean vocals and deep instrumentation processing.

What Can This AI Do?

  • Create songs from scratch in any musical genre — from rap and pop to heavy rock and jazz.
  • Generate realistic vocals in multiple languages, including Russian, perfectly conveying intonations.
  • Write lyrics on a given topic or use the user's ready-made text for a song.
  • Allows splitting the track into stems, extending the composition, and making precise remixes of specific sections.

How to Use It for Free?

After a quick registration, Udio provides users with a monthly package of free credits. This limit is more than enough to experiment with styles and create your own full musical album.
