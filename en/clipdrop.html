Clipdrop: An AI-Powered Pocket Photo Studio

Clipdrop is an ecosystem of "smart" tools for quick graphics processing created by the developers of the famous Stable Diffusion neural network. The service is perfect for marketplaces, bloggers, and designers.

What's Included in the Free Toolkit?

  • Remove Background: Perfect and instant background removal from complex objects (even flyaway hair).
  • Cleanup: A smart "eraser." Mask out any unwanted object, blemish, or watermark on a photo, and the AI will remove it trace-free while recreating the background texture.
  • Uncrop: Aspect ratio expansion. If your photo doesn't fit into an Instagram square or a cover format, the AI will imagine and build out the surroundings on its own.
