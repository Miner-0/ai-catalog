CapCut AI: A Revolution in Mobile and Desktop Video Editing

CapCut has long ceased to be just a simple video clipping app. Today, it is a powerhouse packed with built-in AI tools that automate the most tedious editing processes on both phones and computers.

Top 3 AI Features in CapCut:

  • Auto Captions: AI recognizes human speech in videos in 20+ languages and transforms it into beautiful, animated on-screen text within seconds.
  • Smart Chroma Key Without Green Screen: The AI-powered "Background Removal" feature can cleanly cut a person out of the frame, even if the video was shot in a regular room.
  • AI Avatars & Voiceover: You can type text, select a digital character (presenter), and they will realistically read out your script with a voiceover in the video.
