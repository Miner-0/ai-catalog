Flux AI: The New Standard for Photorealistic Graphics

Flux AI is a cutting-edge open-source image generation neural network created by the engineers who once developed the legendary Stable Diffusion. The model surpasses many paid alternatives in terms of detail.

What can this AI do?

  • Generate images with an extreme level of realism (skin, object textures, lighting).
  • Perfectiy integrate legible printed text inside images upon user request.
  • Render anatomically correct hands, fingers, and complex human poses.
  • Strictly follow the details of even very long and complex prompts.

How to use it for free?

Since the model is open-source, it can be tested absolutely free of charge without limitations on numerous public platforms and demo hostings (for example, Hugging Face, Fal AI, or through the free interfaces of third-party AI generators).
