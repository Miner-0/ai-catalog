Viggle AI: Viral Character Animation and Video Meme Creation

Viggle AI is a specialized neural network for controllable motion capture that has taken social media by storm. With its help, you can take any image and accurately transfer the physics of movement from a video template onto it.

What Can This AI Do?

  • “Bring to life” static characters, making them dance, jump, or perform complex stunts.
  • Perfectly replace a person in a video with any other character while maintaining realistic tracking of clothing and limbs.
  • Create hilarious video memes in a matter of seconds.
  • Generate character animation based on a text description of movements.

How to Use It for Free?

The neural network is actively evolving and provides free access to its capabilities through a user-friendly web application or the project's official Discord server. Users are granted daily free attempts to render funny and high-quality videos.
