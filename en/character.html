Character.AI: Virtual Companions and AI Roleplay Chats

Character.AI is a unique web service that allows you to engage in text dialogues with artificial intelligence programmed to match a specific personality or character. The project enjoys immense popularity worldwide.

What can this AI do?

  • Imitate conversations with real historical or public figures (like Elon Musk or Albert Einstein).
  • Chat on behalf of popular fictional characters from games, anime, movies, and books.
  • Act as a personal coach, psychologist, language tutor, or text adventure game narrator.
  • Allow you to create your own bot in just a couple of clicks by setting its personality, voice, and manner of speech.

How to use it for free?

The platform is completely free to use. Users get unlimited messaging with all bots on the website. The service features excellent multilingual comprehension and is available both in web browsers and via the official mobile app.
