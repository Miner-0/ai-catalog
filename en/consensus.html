Consensus: AI-Powered Search for Scientific Research

Consensus is an innovative search engine created specifically for fact-checking. Unlike standard chatbots that can hallucinate information, Consensus builds its answers exclusively on the basis of peer-reviewed scientific papers.

What can this AI do?

  • Instantly find answers to complex questions in fields like medicine, physics, psychology, economics, and technology.
  • Analyze hundreds of discovered scientific articles and display a general "consensus" among scientists (for example, what percentage of studies support a hypothesis).
  • Compile brief, structured summaries with links to DOI primary sources.
  • Write texts and essays backed by authoritative sources.

How to use it for free?

The service provides a very convenient free plan, allowing users to make basic search queries without limitations. The AI recognizes queries in Russian and delivers highly accurate academic results.
