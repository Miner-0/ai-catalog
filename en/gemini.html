Google Gemini: Smart AI Integrated with Google Services

Google Gemini (formerly Bard) is Google's main response to the popularity of ChatGPT. The neural network is deeply integrated with the internet and can pull the freshest information in real time through the Google search engine.

Key Advantages of Gemini:

  • Native Multimodality: The AI excels at simultaneously analyzing text, audio tracks, programming code, and complex charts. You can upload a screenshot of a table, and it will instantly convert it into an Excel format.
  • YouTube Analysis: You can send the AI a link to an hour-long YouTube video and ask: "Summarize what this is about and list the main bullet points." The AI will do it in seconds.
  • Free Access: The basic and very fast version of Gemini is available completely free of charge in the web version and as a convenient smartphone app.
